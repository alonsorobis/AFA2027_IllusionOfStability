## 2. Actions

### Stage A — Method review

Invoked `project-manager` and `reviewer-method`. The method review flagged three substantive issues plus seven minor items:

- **Issue 1**: the K-class structure does not earn its keep in the calibration. The calibrated per-class redemption rates are nearly uniform (0.98 across all classes in stress), and the corridor differentiation comes from the class-specific congestion coefficient rather than from class-specific run risk. The abstract claim "noisiest signal endogenously redeems most" is not supported by the calibrated model and needed to be dropped.
- **Issue 2**: the wholesale null is a tautology of the assumed $c_q^{\text{wholesale}} = 1.00$. The framing as a "result" is misleading; the corridor is best presented as a counterfactual conditional on the settlement-finality assumption.
- **Issue 3**: the proof of Proposition 2 contains unedited drafting language ("Wait, this gives... we re-examine the sign...") that must be removed before any external circulation.

All three fixed:
- Abstract re-claimed the third structural result as the convenience-yield comparative static (Proposition 3) rather than the threshold-ordering claim. Introduction rewritten to flag that the calibrated model produces nearly uniform per-class conversion rates and that the segmentation reflects the class-specific congestion coefficient.
- Wholesale paragraph in §6 and §7 re-framed: the wholesale corridor is "best read as a counterfactual reflection of the settlement-finality requirement of wholesale flows rather than as a derived prediction of the model".
- Proof of Proposition 2 rewritten with a Topkis monotone comparative statics argument; the signs are now consistent with the calibration test, and the working-notes language is removed.

Accessibility pass (in line with the author's earlier instruction to lower the jargon load in interpretive sections): the introduction and conclusion now use "convert at the issuer" and "sell on an exchange" instead of "primary redemption" and "secondary-market sale"; "type" replaces "class" in the interpretive sections; "K-class" replaced with "type-heterogeneous" or "heterogeneous-type" in the literature and discussion.
