## 5. Calibration bootstrap (in flight)

Built `bootstrap_calibration.py`: the headline USDC premium is a calibration to a single 96-hour stress window (SVB, March 2023). The window was reconstructed from the CryptoCompare parquet and reproduces the stored targets exactly (2023-03-10 00:00 to 2023-03-13 23:00, min 0.9022, mean-abs 240.06 bps, freq 0.3958). Each replication circular-block-resamples that window (12-hour blocks), recomputes the three stress moments, re-runs the calibration warm-started at $\phi^*$ (Nelder-Mead, maxiter 80, n_draws 150), and recomputes $\Pi = \ell_C + (1-p_s)\ell_{D,b} + p_s\ell_{D,s}$. Baseline moments are held fixed (8,760 hours, quasi-deterministic) and Monte Carlo seeds are fixed across replications so the spread reflects data resampling, not simulation noise. Launched N=40 in the background; the SE and percentile CI will replace the assembled band sentence in §5.3 when it completes.
