## 2. Actions

1. **Confirmed Morris-Shin endogenous fixed point.** Walked through the indifference condition $\mathbb{E}_{\theta|s_i=s^*}[V_R(D(\theta;s^*)) - V_H(\theta)] = 0$ where $s^*$ appears both as the conditioning event of the posterior and as the argument of $D$ that aggregates other holders' decisions. Newton-Krylov solves on the K-dimensional system; Gauss-Hermite quadrature on the indifference integral. Contraction verified numerically (max row sum 0.008).
2. **1-class representative-holder calibration `simple_calibration.py`** with 7 free parameters (sigma, psi, nu, mu_q, zeta, mu_s, tau_s) and 2 externally fixed (theta_baseline=1, tau_baseline=400). USDC issuer params h=0.01, xi=0.20. Background run: warmup 6.31 min, main 22.79 min, loss 4.99. phi* = (0.270, 0.295, 1.852, 0.050, 0.537, 0.204, 8.54). Risk components: counterparty 20, depeg baseline 39.3, depeg stress 235 -> Pi_risk = 61.4 bps at p_stress=0.0108.
3. **Jacobian SVD identification check on simple phi***. 5/7 well identified (nu 10.4, sigma 5.8, psi 1.4, zeta 1.2, mu_q 1.2), 2/7 marginal (mu_s 0.31, tau_s 0.16). Effective rank 3 of 6 singular values. Honestly reported but author rejected the "weak identification" framing.
4. **Sensitivity panel on (mu_s, tau_s) bounds.** 8 perturbations to parameter-interior bounds. Headline span [58.9, 61.7] bps, range 2.78 bps = 4.5% of central. Robust headline demonstrated.
5. **Comprehensive payments cost database `payments_cost_database.json`** (414 lines, IJCB-level). 20+ rails: PIX, FedNow, SEPA Instant, UPI, Faster Payments, SPEI, BIS Nexus, mBridge, Agora, SWIFT correspondent, Wise, Western Union, MoneyGram, Visa/Mastercard cross-border, USDT/USDC on Tron/Ethereum/Solana. Sourced from BCB, NPCI, FRB, EPC, Pay.UK, BIS, McKinsey, World Bank RPW, Chainalysis, Tronsave.
6. **Empirical comparison framework `empirical_comparison.py`** with three use cases: BR domestic $40, US-MX $400, EU-BR $1000 (pre/post BCB package). Nexus overlay. Initial results with single USDC-calibrated premium 61 bps: USDT dominated by PIX at $40, wins by 381 over Wise at $400, wins by 413 over SWIFT at $1000 pre-package.
