## 2. Decision: descriptive cross-section, no extra structural calibration

The author's worry is right: structurally calibrating more coins is the wrong battle (DAI+USDT joint calibration failed earlier because DAI is crypto-collateralised, a different mechanism). Resolution: keep the structural calibration to USDC and USDT (the two largest, ~90% of the segment, the two cleanest opposite profiles), and address the referee's "N=2" with a *descriptive* cross-section of the broader fiat-backed set, with no extra model.

Data access: the keyless price endpoints that worked on 2026-06-01 (CoinGecko, CryptoCompare) are now gated (401/429).
