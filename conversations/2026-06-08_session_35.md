## 2. Tronscan fetch and a data-quality finding

Confirmed Tronscan has a free tier and is reachable. `simulation/scripts/fetch_tron_redemptions.py` pulled USDT TRC-20 transfers involving the zero address (mint = from zero, burn = to zero) over the Terra window: **only 4 events, $1bn mint + $4bn burn = $3bn net redemption**. Cross-checked against DefiLlama's Tron USDT circulating series, which shows a **~$8bn contraction** over the same window. The zero-address scan therefore **undercounts** Tron redemptions by more than half (Tether's Tron mint/burn passes partly outside zero-address Transfer events). The key was still useful: it let us diagnose the undercount. **Conclusion: for Tron, DefiLlama's daily per-chain supply contraction is the complete redemption measure**, not the on-chain zero-address scan.

## 3. The USDT/Terra twin figure

`simulation/scripts/make_fig_terra_onchain.py` -> `paper/figures/fig_terra_onchain.pdf` (and `data/processed/tron_usdt_supply_daily.csv`): USDT hourly secondary price (below-par depeg shaded) against daily net redemption measured as the contraction of USDT's Tron circulating supply (DefiLlama). Window net redemption $8.4bn; minimum USDT price 0.9751.

**The figure is the mirror image of the USDC/SVB one, and that contrast is the point.** USDT's primary redemption stayed open: ~$8bn was redeemed at par (large bars during the stress) and the depeg was a brief dip to 0.975 that recovered within two days. USDC's redemption was suspended over the SVB weekend, so its depeg was deep (0.90) and the at-par burns came only afterward. The depeg is severe when primary redemption is impaired and mild when the channel absorbs the outflow -- the model's mechanism in two episodes. Added as Figure~\ref{fig:terra_onchain} in §5.4 with a framing paragraph (and a one-line note that the redemption is measured via supply contraction because the zero-address scan undercounts Tron mint/burn).
