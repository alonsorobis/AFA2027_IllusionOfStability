## 3. Methodological discussion (advisory; no model change made)

Author asked whether the paper could have been solved in closed form and whether that would make sense. Assessment given: a closed form is available only as a stripped special case (uniform noise / σ→0, linear price impact, decoupled counterparty–depeg risks, single regime); each simplification removes a feature the paper exists to keep (the calibrated signal-noise σ that separates USDC from USDT, the convex stress-regime depeg, the counterparty–depeg coupling, the per-component bps decomposition). The qualitative content is *already* analytical — Propositions 1–3 (uniqueness via contraction; reserve and convenience-yield comparative-static signs) are closed-form; only the *magnitude* (61/89 bps) needs numerics, which is the standard "signs analytical, levels calibrated" division. Closed-form vs numerical is downstream of the question: Klee et al. want a testable qualitative regime-switching prediction (closed form is apt), the paper wants a calibrated, decomposed price (numerical by nature). **Offered, not done:** a short nested closed-form special case in an appendix as a transparent benchmark that collapses to a known global-game threshold when convexity and coupling are switched off.
