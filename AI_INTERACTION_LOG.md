5. **Human work outside Claude Code sessions** (manual data downloads, paper reading in PDF viewer, hand edits in Word/Overleaf) is logged in `HUMAN_TIME_LOG.md`, not here.
6. **This stablecoins paper is logged by default.** Every Claude Code session that touches the paper is recorded here, even short ones; the assistant proposes the row and transcript at the end of the session.
