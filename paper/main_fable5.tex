\begin{abstract}
\noindent Sending a fiat-backed stablecoin costs a fraction of a correspondent-banking payment, but the fee understates the cost the sender bears: the holder is exposed to issuer counterparty risk and to secondary-market price dispersion when par convertibility breaks. We price both with a global game calibrated separately to the March 2023 USDC and May 2022 USDT episodes, obtaining premiums of 49 and 71 basis points with inverted compositions. Across retail corridors, the stablecoin path is dominated where a public instant-payment rail already exists, wins where none does, and is absorbed once an upgraded public cross-border rail goes live.
\end{abstract}
