\noindent This internet appendix is the comprehensive companion to the short working paper ``The Risk-Adjusted Cost of Stablecoins as Payment Instruments.'' The working paper states the argument schematically; everything technical lives here. It collects, in order: the formal global-game model and its propositions (Appendix~\ref{app:formal_model}); the calibration and identification diagnostics (Appendix~\ref{app:calib_details}); the regulatory-uncertainty band, the counterparty-parameter sensitivity, and the residual diagnostics (Appendix~\ref{app:sensitivity}); the consolidated use-case summary (Appendix~\ref{app:empirical_summary}); and supplementary descriptive evidence, a retail-attention cross-check, and a two-class robustness extension. Cross-references of the form ``Section~X'' point to the working paper; the risk premium $\Pi_{\text{risk}}$, the calibrated vector $\phi^*$, and the use-case rankings are defined there.
