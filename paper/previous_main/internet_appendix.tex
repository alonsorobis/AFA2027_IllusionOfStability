\noindent This internet appendix collects supplementary material for ``The Risk-Adjusted Cost of Stablecoins as Payment Instruments: An International Comparison.'' It contains (i) descriptive evidence on stablecoin pricing around recent regulatory enactments, (ii) a retail-attention cross-check based on Google Trends search volume, and (iii) a two-class robustness extension of the global game that maps the deepest portion of the stress-regime trough onto the secondary-market intermediation sector. References to ``the main paper'' point to the published article; the structural risk premium $\Pi_{\text{risk}}$, the calibrated parameter vector $\phi^*$, and the use-case rankings are defined there.
