\section{AI workflow and reproducibility}\label{app:ai_workflow}

This paper was prepared in compliance with the AFA 2027 Special Session call for papers on works produced with generative AI workflows. The investigation began on 1 June 2026, which is the earliest date permitted by the call. All AI sessions are logged in the project's interaction index, with verbatim author prompts and a chronological audit of AI tool calls. A time log of human activity, an authorship report tracking the share of project lines produced by the author versus the AI assistant, and the full conversation transcripts are provided as part of the submission package under the headings \texttt{AI\_INTERACTION\_LOG.md}, \texttt{HUMAN\_TIME\_LOG.md}, \texttt{AUTHORSHIP\_REPORT.md} and \texttt{conversations/}.

The AI assistant used throughout the project was Claude Code (Anthropic), accessed through the VSCode native extension, running model Opus 4.7 for the initial sessions and Opus 4.8 thereafter. The assistant was directed by the author at every substantive decision point: research question selection, scope rulings, calibration-event selection, corridor mapping, model specification, falsification logic, and presentation choices. Background sub-agents were used for two task types: bulk data downloads and Monte Carlo calibration runs. The full prompts that triggered each sub-agent are reproduced in the corresponding session transcripts.

The simulation code is implemented in Python (3.14) inside a dedicated virtual environment, with dependencies pinned in \texttt{simulation/requirements.txt}. The fixed-point solver uses \texttt{scipy.optimize.root} with the Krylov method, vectorised over Gauss-Hermite quadrature nodes for the indifference condition. The calibration uses \texttt{scipy.optimize.minimize} with Nelder-Mead and bound clipping, with a two-pass procedure that warms up on a small sample and refines on a larger one. The full unit test suite is included in \texttt{simulation/tests/}; at the time of the calibration reported here, all unit tests pass.

Data downloads are reproducible from the included scripts in \texttt{data/scripts/}. Each download is recorded in \texttt{data/download\_log.md} with timestamp, source URL, output path and SHA-256 hash. The Banco Central do Brasil primary materials used in Section~\ref{sec:landscape} and Section~\ref{sec:discussion} are stored in \texttt{data/raw/manual/} with the same provenance discipline.

The author confirms that, except for the assistance of the AI system named above, no other human contributed work to this submission. The cumulative time investment of the author is recorded in \texttt{HUMAN\_TIME\_LOG.md} and is dominated by reading, ruling on decision points, and reviewing AI output.
