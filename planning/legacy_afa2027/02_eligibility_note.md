# Eligibility note — interpretation of the AFA rule on start date

The AFA 2027 special-session call states that **investigations must begin on
or after 2026-06-01**. This note is about whether the present plan, which
carries over an existing paper submitted in March 2026 to Human x AI Finance,
complies with that rule.

## The two readings of the rule

**Strict reading.** "Investigation" means the research project. If the paper
already exists, the investigation has started before June 1 and the
submission is ineligible.

**Substantive reading.** The special session is about generative-AI
workflows. The "investigation" is the new AI-augmented research effort that
the workflow log documents. Prior work is allowed as background as long as
the new submission is materially new, the AI workflow is documented from
2026-06-01 onward, and the cover letter is transparent about both.

Neither reading is explicitly endorsed by the call text. The conservative
move is to email the organisers and ask before submission. The realistic
move, given the timing, is to proceed under the substantive reading and
disclose openly.

## How we plan to be compliant under the substantive reading

1. **Start the AI workflow log on 2026-06-01.** Every interaction with
   Claude Code, ChatGPT, or any other AI tool from that date is logged in
   `log/` using the format documented in `03_workflow_log_format.md`. Prior
   AI use that produced the HxAI version is acknowledged but not claimed as
   AFA-eligible workflow.
2. **Open the June 1 session by declaring the starting point.** The first
   log entry will record that we are taking the HxAI version as input
   material and starting a fresh investigation aimed at resolving the
   specific weaknesses identified by the HxAI referees. That makes the new
   investigation traceable.
3. **The substantive contribution must be genuinely new**, not a cosmetic
   revision. The three fixes (Morris-Shin fixed point, tornado sensitivity,
   external empirical anchor) and the rewrite of the paper around the
   "illusion of stability" framing are the new investigation. If by end of
   July those fixes are not delivered, we do not submit. See the stop-loss
   in `00_action_plan.md`.
4. **Cover letter must be transparent**. The submission to AFA will state,
   in the cover letter: "A previous version of this material was submitted
   to the Human x AI Finance conference in March 2026 and was not accepted.
   The present submission is a substantive revision developed entirely with
   generative AI workflows beginning on 2026-06-01, in response to the
   referee report received from that prior submission. The complete AI
   workflow log is attached as supplementary material."

## Optional safer path

Email the AFA special-session organisers before 2026-06-15 with a short
description of the situation and ask explicitly whether prior submission to
a non-finance conference disqualifies the present project. If they say no,
we submit. If they say yes, we re-route to JEDC. If they do not respond by
mid July, we proceed under the substantive reading with the cover-letter
disclosure described above.

Recommendation: send the email in the first week of June. The downside of
not asking is finding out at the wrong moment.

## Worst case

If the AFA paper is judged ineligible after submission, the work is not
wasted. The same paper, with the same upgrades, is a strong fit for the
Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control, the Journal of Financial Stability,
the BIS Working Paper series, or a Banco de Espana Documento de Trabajo. The
timeline of "complete the upgrades by end of August 2026, then submit
elsewhere with the AI workflow log as a methodological appendix" remains
attractive regardless of the AFA outcome.
