# AFA 2027 special session — "Stablecoins and the Illusion of Stability" v2

This folder hosts the work to revise and resubmit the stablecoin paper for the
AFA 2027 special session on papers written with generative AI workflows.

**Status as of 2026-05-28**: pre-launch. Active work begins **2026-06-01** to
comply with the AFA rule that investigations must begin on or after that date.

## Where things live

- `00_action_plan.md` — the 3-month plan (June, July, August 2026) with weekly
  deliverables and a stop-loss point at end of June.
- `01_starting_state.md` — what we have today and what is being carried over
  from the prior HxAI Finance submission.
- `02_eligibility_note.md` — interpretation of the AFA rule on start date and
  the recommended way to present the prior submission in the cover letter.
- `03_workflow_log_format.md` — the format for the AI-workflow log required by
  the AFA call.
- `log/` — daily entries starting 2026-06-01.

## What we are not doing

- We are not starting a brand new paper. The substantive contribution is
  carried over: an agent-based model of stablecoin fragility under stress,
  calibrated to the March 2023 USDC episode, with cross-border users
  identified as both the most payment-useful and the most redemption-sensitive
  segment.
- We are not rewriting the model from scratch. We are fixing the three real
  weaknesses flagged by the HxAI Finance reviewers and documenting an AI
  workflow that begins 2026-06-01.
